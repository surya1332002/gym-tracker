What this can prove

    
The readout identifies the running display mode and safe insets reported by this browser. It cannot read Chrome feature flags or prove that pixels are drawn behind the Android clock/camera cutout. A zero top inset can mean the viewport is already below the status bar; it is not permission to move controls upwards.

    
Keep the Android clock, camera, battery and gesture controls visible. Compare their placement on the actual phone. Do not enable immersive/fullscreen mode or clear app storage to change this result.

    
This page reads browser/display information only. It does not access account data, tokens, workout storage or Supabase, and sends no diagnostic report anywhere.
